# Editions and metadata

**Book metadata (META-1).** The Details panel shows the book's details from the source (title,
subtitle, author or pen name, language, series, publisher, rights, description) and lets you add
Book's own: contributors and their roles, subject codes, the imprint, the copyright holder, other
books by the author and an about-the-author text, or override a source value for Book's outputs
only. What a store or printer would miss is listed under *Needs*.

**Editions (META-2).** A book starts with a paperback and an ebook. Each edition has its own ISBN
(checked as you type: a mistyped digit is caught), and a paperback its trim, paper and print
profile; an ebook says whether to embed fonts, how notes show and which cover to use. Design
values can differ per edition.
