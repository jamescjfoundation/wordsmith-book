# Wordsmith Book guide

Wordsmith Book turns a finished manuscript into a print-ready PDF for a paperback or hardcover and
a valid, accessible EPUB for ebook stores, and checks every word of both against the source. Its
interface is in English and Spanish.

1. [Installing and updating](installing.md): the download for your computer, how to open it, and what the update check asks.
2. [Getting started](getting-started.md): a new book, the window, and the way from a manuscript to finished files.
3. [Importing a manuscript](import.md): a linked Manuscript project, or a document imported with a review, a cleanup and a word check.
4. [Structure: chapters, parts, front and back matter](structure.md): the outline, numbering, the pages Book makes and light fixes.
5. [Designs](designs.md): thirteen designs, mixing them, your own, and the chooser.
6. [Print](print.md): trims, margins, paper, bleed, notes, line breaks and hyphens, covers, large print and the print PDF.
7. [Ebook](ebook.md): the EPUB, its navigation, accessibility, fonts, cover, notes and page list.
8. [Previews and preflight](preview-and-preflight.md): print spreads, the ebook on a device, and the checks before export.
9. [Export](export.md): the checked files, the project bundle, proofs and the ONIX record.
10. [Editions and metadata](editions-and-metadata.md): the book's details, its editions and store editions.
11. [Working with Wordsmith Manuscript](manuscript.md): a linked manuscript, fixes offered back, and versions.
12. [Files, saving and backups](files.md): the project folder, the packed file, recovery, backups and other versions of the app.
13. [A publishing house](house.md): the house file, house styles, imprints, series and ISBNs.
14. [Keys](keys.md): the default key for every command.
15. [Command line and agents](command-line-and-agents.md): the app's work without its window, and its commands as tools for an agent.
16. [Feature ids](feature-ids.md): for people working on Book, where the guide describes each feature of the specification.
